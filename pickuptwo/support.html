Learning to play

The app has a step-by-step tutorial. You can replay it any time from Profile → How to play.

Signing in

Sign in the same way you did the first time to get back to the same account and progress.

Reporting a player

In a game's chat you can report a message or mute a player. If something needs our attention, email us with the player's name.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete account, or email us and we'll do it for you.
