Pick Up Two – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who we are

Pick Up Two is made by IVYMIC LABS LTD, a company registered in England and Wales. Contact: support@ivymiclabs.com.

What we collect

Account details (email, sign-in method, display name), gameplay and chat data, device and technical data (including push tokens, crash reports and logs), and ad-related data.

How we use it

To provide and improve the game, keep it secure and send notifications. Our legal bases are contract, legitimate interests and, for notifications and personalised ads, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Ads

We use Google AdMob to show ads. Google and its partners may collect device data, including advertising identifiers, and show personalised ads only with your consent. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can change your ad choices in the app and in your device settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the app, some of which may process it outside the UK under appropriate safeguards, and where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account, except where we must keep it by law. You can delete your account in the app or by emailing us.

Children

You must be 13 or older to use Pick Up Two.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its use, by emailing support@ivymiclabs.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
