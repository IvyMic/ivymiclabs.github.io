FailForGood

Fail your goals, fund a good cause.

FailForGood is a habit-accountability app for iOS and Android, made by IvyMicLabs Ltd. You set a goal-based challenge with friends — go to the gym three times a week, finish that essay, run a 10k — submit proof when you complete it, and judge each other's results.

How stakes work

Some challenges let you add an optional money stake to keep yourself honest:

  1. When you join a staked challenge, you save a payment card. Nothing is charged yet.
  2. If you complete your goal, you pay nothing — ever.
  3. If you fail, you confirm your stake payment in the app. The money goes to a charity chosen by the challenge winners (or to supporting FailForGood, if a participant chose that option), minus a platform fee that keeps the service running.

You can never win money on FailForGood. Winners choose where the losers' stakes go — stakes are never paid out to players. FailForGood is not gambling, betting, or a prize competition.
